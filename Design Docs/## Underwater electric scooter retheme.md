## Underwater electric scooter retheme

##Theme elements
Ride an electric scooter underwater, and overcome bizarre sea creatures, startin with a Clownfish with a Chainsaw, and ending with the Great White Shark. Maybe in the middle there's a Kraken takin' a break'n.

##Mechanical Changes Requested by Archie that go with this
1/ Pick up hand after defeating a fish
2/ He really wanted to just beat the fish as soon as he could, rather than develop his hand
3/ Want to get gems right at the end before he leaves


##Possible Solution - Oxygen System

The player has [5] oxygen, and the run ends if it runs out. (We could use face-down cards to represent Oxygen)

Each action costs 1 oxygen. This includes 
- gaining a card from the market
- picking up your hand
- refreshing the market
- gaining a gem
- attacking a fish

###Refreshes after beating fish
When a fish is beaten, the player gets +X Oxygen, (maybe all of it? Or maybe like +3 or whatever is written on the card) and they pick up their hand.

So yeah maybe the fish actually shows their reward on the card to make it really clear, like +3 Oxygen, Reveal 1 Gem, Pick up Hand. Others might refresh the market.
