Treasure Experiment

Goals
* Give player a goal other than survival
* Reduce the number of number cards in hand
* Give a progress metric that players can improve over runs
* Bring in a bit more variation
* Give another use of the "target" idea, where you have to exactly match
* Give some more challenging targets, and cool uses of negative numbers


Proposed rules
* Treasure appears mixed in with the market, and is approximately 1/4 of the cards
* 1/3 of the treasure uses negative numbers as Targets
* 1/3 are square numbers as Targets, 4,9,16,25,36,49,64,81
* 1/3 are highly composite numbers as Targets, 6,12,18,24,30,36,42,48,54,60,66,72,78,84,90,96
* Treasure cards are are a gem, and would just show a gem icon. They have 1,2 or 3 gems on them.
